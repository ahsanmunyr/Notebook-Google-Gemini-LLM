Title: The Lantern Road

## Style
- Point of view: third person limited, following the protagonist.
- Tense: past tense.
- Tone: warm but tense; grounded, cinematic, sensory.
- Length: aim for 1,500–2,500 words per chapter.
- Formatting: prose only, no headings or lists. Use "***" for scene breaks.
- End most chapters on a hook. Never contradict the "story so far".

## Characters
- Mara Vance — 34, cartographer, stubborn, dry humor. Wants to find her missing brother.
- Elias — 40s, a lighthouse keeper who knows more than he admits.

## World
- A coastal country where roads are lit by old lanterns that must never go dark.
- Present-dayish, faintly magical. Keep the magic subtle and consistent.

## Outline
- Act 1 (ch 1–3): Mara learns her brother vanished on the Lantern Road.
- Act 2 (ch 4–6): She travels the road and uncovers why the lanterns are failing.
- Act 3 (ch 7+): The truth, the cost, and the choice.
